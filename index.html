Six self-contained worlds where a handful of tiny rules become something that looks alive. No libraries, no network, no assets — each is a single HTML file you can save, share or pull apart. They are a small museum of the ways order makes itself: out of forces, out of growth, out of scent-trails, and out of alignment.
